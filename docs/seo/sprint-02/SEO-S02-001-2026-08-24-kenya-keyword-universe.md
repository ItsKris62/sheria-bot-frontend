## 3. Key Findings & Strategic Summary

1. **Mandatory Compliance Triggers Drive Highest Intent:**
   In Kenya, the most commercially valuable searches are driven by mandatory statutory deadlines and licensing gates:
   * CBK DCP Licensing (Form CBK DCP 1)
   * CBK PSP Licensing (Electronic Retail & E-Money)
   * ODPC Mandatory Data Controller/Processor Registration
   * Annual DCP Regulatory Fees & Compliance Returns (December 31)
2. **Information Scarcity on Practical Operational Execution:**
   Regulator websites publish the acts and PDF gazettes, but provide zero interactive gap analysis, automated policy drafting, or dynamic checklists.
3. **Law Firm Dominance on Top-of-Funnel Blog Commentary:**
   Kenyan law firms (Bowmans, Coulson Harney, CM Advocates, MMAN, Anjarwalla & Khanna) rank for high-level explainers, but lack software automation. SheriaBot's primary differentiator is converting informational law search traffic directly into software action.
