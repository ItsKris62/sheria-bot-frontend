## 1. YMYL Regulatory Accuracy Standards

As an AI-powered regulatory intelligence platform covering legal, banking, and data protection compliance (Your Money or Your Life — YMYL), SheriaBot enforces rigorous editorial governance:

1. **Statutory Primacy:** Every legal statement must be grounded in primary statutory instruments (Acts of Parliament, gazetted regulations, regulatory policy guidance notes, or official circulars).
2. **Honest Attribution:** Content is attributed transparently to the *SheriaBot Regulatory Intelligence Team* or *SheriaBot Editorial Team*. No fabricated individual attorney personas or fictional qualifications may be published.
3. **Statutory Disclaimers:** Every public guide must contain a clear statutory disclaimer indicating that the intelligence provided constitutes educational analysis based on current legislation and does not replace formal legal counsel.
4. **Current-Law Labeling:** Proposed bills, consultative papers, and pending amendments must be clearly marked as `PROPOSED` or `UNDER CONSULTATION` and never represented as enforceable law.
