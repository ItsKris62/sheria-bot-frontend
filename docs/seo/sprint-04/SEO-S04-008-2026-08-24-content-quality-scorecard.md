## 1. Scorecard Dimensions & Weighting

| Evaluation Dimension | Max Points | Evaluation Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Evidence & Statutory Grounding** | **20** | All factual legal claims map to primary Acts, gazettes, or official circulars. |
| **2. Search Intent Satisfaction** | **20** | Quick answer, comprehensive answers, and distinct intent coverage. |
| **3. Original Practical Value** | **15** | Includes actionable checklists, timelines, decision trees, or comparison matrices. |
| **4. Topical Depth & Clarity** | **15** | Structured headings, clear readability, logical breakdown of complex statutory rules. |
| **5. Internal Linking & Architecture** | **10** | Upward link to pillar, contextual lateral links, and product conversion bridge. |
| **6. Metadata & Technical Quality** | **10** | Unique title, descriptive meta description, self-referencing canonical, and JSON-LD. |
| **7. Conversion & Product Relevance** | **10** | Contextual relevance to SheriaBot intelligence tools without aggressive gating. |
| **Total Possible Score** | **100** | Minimum publication threshold: **80 / 100** |

---

## 2. Mandatory Evidence Veto Rule

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Regardless of the aggregate score, any article containing unverified legal claims, stale statutory citations (e.g. `Cap 59B`, `7 days STR`, `US$10,000 CTR`), or unevidenced deadlines receives an **AUTOMATIC FAIL (Veto)** and cannot be published until remediated.
