## 1. Decision Framework: "Update Existing Pillar" vs "New Blog Article"

When an official regulatory change is gazetted or issued by the CBK, ODPC, FRC, or CMA:

```text
                               [ Regulatory Event Detected ]
                                             │
                       Does it alter an existing statutory rule
                      without creating distinct standalone intent?
                                      /              \
                                   YES                NO
                                   /                    \
              [ Update Existing Authority Page ]      [ Create New Blog Article ]
              • Update section text & citations       • Cover breaking timeline
              • Update "Last Reviewed" date           • Deep commentary & impact
              • Update statutory dependency record    • Link to parent pillar
```

---

## 2. Decision Guidelines

1. **Update Existing Authority Page When:**
   - A filing deadline, annual fee, or threshold is amended by gazette notice.
   - The primary search query remains identical (e.g. `CBK DCP annual fee`).
   - Creating a second URL would cause keyword cannibalization.

2. **Publish a New Blog Article When:**
   - The regulatory announcement creates novel search demand (e.g. `CBK circular on digital credit pricing August 2026`).
   - Operators search specifically for the announcement, timeline, or market reaction.
   - The blog post includes a prominent reciprocal link to the canonical authority pillar.
