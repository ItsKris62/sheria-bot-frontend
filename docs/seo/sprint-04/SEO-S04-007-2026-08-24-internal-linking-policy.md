## 1. Hub-and-Spoke Link Architecture

Internal link equity flows hierarchically to reinforce topical depth:

1. **Spoke to Pillar:** Every supporting Knowledge Base guide or blog post must link upward to its parent regulatory pillar (e.g. `/knowledge-base/odpc-data-protection-registration-renewal-kenya` → `/kenya/odpc-data-protection-compliance`).
2. **Pillar to Master Hub:** Every regulatory pillar links upward to `/kenya/fintech-compliance-requirements`.
3. **Master Hub to Pillars:** The Master FinTech Hub links downward to all four specialized pillars.
4. **Pillars to Sibling Spokes:** Pillars link laterally to operational spoke guides.
5. **Product Bridges:** All content pieces feature contextual, non-intrusive conversion CTAs to `/solutions/startups`, `/solutions/enterprise`, or `/pricing`.

---

## 2. Anchor Text Standards

* **Natural, Descriptive Anchors (Approved):**
  - `CBK digital credit provider compliance guide`
  - `ODPC data controller registration renewal requirements`
  - `Kenya AML/CFT and FRC reporting obligations`
  - `Data Protection Impact Assessment methodology`
* **Prohibited Anchors:**
  - Generic terms: `click here`, `read more`, `learn more`, `this guide`.
  - Artificial keyword-stuffed strings: `best fintech compliance Kenya software automated AI platform`.
