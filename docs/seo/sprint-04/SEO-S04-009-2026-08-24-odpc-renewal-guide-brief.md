## 2. Statutory Scope & Evidence
* **Primary Instruments:**
  1. Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021 (Regulations 9 & 11)
  2. Data Protection Act, 2019 (Act No. 24 of 2019)
* **Approved Claims:**
  * Certificate validity is 24 months from issuance under Regulation 9.
  * Renewal applications are submitted under Regulation 11 prior to expiration.
  * Financial services entities must maintain continuous active registration under the Third Schedule.
* **Prohibited Claims:**
  * Do NOT state certificates expire annually.
  * Do NOT cite repealed draft guidelines or incorrect regulation numbers (e.g. 9(3)).
