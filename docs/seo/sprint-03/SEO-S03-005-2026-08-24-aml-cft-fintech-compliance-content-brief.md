## 2. Regulatory Evidence & Statutory Scope
* **Evidence Matrix References:** `POCAMLA / FRC Registration`, `Suspicious Transaction Reporting (STR)`, `Cash Transaction Reporting (CTR)`.
* **Primary Statutory Instruments:**
  1. Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act (POCAMLA Cap. 59A)
  2. Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Regulations, 2023
  3. Financial Reporting Centre (FRC) Compliance Guidelines
* **Approved Legal Claims:**
  * Reporting institutions must register with the FRC under Section 47A POCAMLA Cap. 59A.
  * Suspicious Transaction Reporting (STR): Reporting institutions must file STRs with the FRC within two days after the suspicion arose under Section 44(2) POCAMLA. Strict tipping-off prohibitions apply under Section 49.
  * Cash Transaction Reporting (CTR): Mandatory reporting of cash transactions equivalent to US$15,000 or above in any currency under Section 44(6), Fourth Schedule, and Regulation 40 of POCAMLA Regulations 2023.
  * Institutions must establish Customer Due Diligence (CDD) and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs).
* **Prohibited / Unverified Claims:**
  * Do NOT cite repealed "Cap 59B" or repealed "2013 Regulations".
  * Do NOT cite the obsolete 7-day STR filing window (the statutory requirement is 2 days).
  * Do NOT cite the obsolete US$10,000 CTR threshold as current law.
  * Do NOT convert US$15,000 into a fixed KES amount.
