## 2. Regulatory Evidence & Statutory Scope
* **Evidence Matrix References:** `DPIA Obligation`.
* **Primary Statutory Instruments:**
  1. Data Protection Act, 2019 (Section 31)
  2. Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021 (Regulation 49)
  3. ODPC Guidance Notes on DPIA
* **Approved Legal Claims:**
  * Under Section 31 DPA 2019, a DPIA is mandatory where a processing operation is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of data subjects.
  * Regulation 49 of the General Regulations 2021 defines high-risk triggers including systematic and extensive profiling, automated decision-making with legal effects, and large-scale processing of sensitive data.
  * A DPIA must contain a systematic description of processing operations, assessment of necessity and proportionality, assessment of risks, and risk mitigation measures.
* **Prohibited / Unverified Claims:**
  * Do NOT state that every single data processing task requires a DPIA without meeting the statutory high-risk test.
