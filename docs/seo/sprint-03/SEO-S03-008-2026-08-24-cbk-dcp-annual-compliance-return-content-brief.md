## 2. Regulatory Evidence & Statutory Scope
* **Evidence Matrix References:** `DCP Annual Fee`, `DCP Annual Compliance Return`.
* **Primary Statutory Instruments:**
  1. Central Bank of Kenya (Digital Credit Providers) Regulations, 2022
  2. CBK Prudential Guidelines & Circulars
* **Approved Legal Claims:**
  * Under Regulation 5(5), a DCP licence is perpetual unless suspended or revoked.
  * Under Regulation 5(6) and the Second Schedule, a licensed DCP must pay an annual regulatory fee of KES 20,000 to the Central Bank of Kenya on or before 31 December every year.
  * Under Regulation 5(7), a licensed DCP must submit an annual return certifying compliance with the Act and Regulations on or before 31 December every year.
  * Non-compliance with fee payments or return submissions exposes the licensee to regulatory sanctions under Regulation 35 and potential licence suspension.
* **Prohibited / Unverified Claims:**
  * Do NOT use "licence renewal" terminology implying licence re-issuance.
  * Do NOT cite incorrect regulations (5(1), 5(2), 5(3), or 11).
