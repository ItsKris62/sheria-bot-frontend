## 2. Regulatory Evidence & Statutory Scope
* **Evidence Matrix References:** `DCP Licensing`, `ODPC Registration`, `POCAMLA Registration`, `CMA Sandbox`, `Investment Crowdfunding`.
* **Primary Statutory Instruments:**
  1. Central Bank of Kenya Act (Cap. 491) & CBK (Digital Credit Providers) Regulations, 2022
  2. Data Protection Act, 2019 (Act No. 24 of 2019) & Data Protection (Registration) Regulations, 2021
  3. Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act (POCAMLA Cap. 59A) & POCAMLA Regulations, 2023
  4. Capital Markets Act (Cap. 485A) & CMA (Regulatory Sandbox) Policy Guidance Note, 2019
* **Approved Legal Claims:**
  * Multi-regulator oversight applies conditionally depending on product classification (credit, payments, data processing, capital market innovation).
  * DCP licensing is mandatory for non-deposit credit providers under CBK DCP Regs 2022.
  * ODPC small-entity exemption does not apply where processing falls within Third Schedule mandatory sectors, including financial services.
  * FRC reporting institution registration is mandatory under Section 47A POCAMLA Cap. 59A.
  * Suspicious transactions must be reported within two days after suspicion arose under Section 44(2) POCAMLA.
* **Prohibited / Unverified Claims:**
  * Do NOT state all fintechs are regulated by CBK.
  * Do NOT state all fintechs require payment provider licensing.
  * Do NOT publish unverified quantitative PSP capital adequacy figures.
  * Do NOT use obsolete Cap 59B, 7-day STR, or US$10,000 CTR citations.
