## 2. Regulatory Evidence & Statutory Scope
* **Evidence Matrix References:** `DCP Licensing`, `DCP Annual Fee`, `DCP Annual Compliance Return`.
* **Primary Statutory Instruments:**
  1. Central Bank of Kenya Act (Cap. 491)
  2. Central Bank of Kenya (Digital Credit Providers) Regulations, 2022 (*Legal Notice No. 46 of 2022*)
  3. CBK Prudential Guidelines on Fit and Proper Requirements (CBK/PG/02)
* **Approved Legal Claims:**
  * All digital credit providers carrying on digital credit business must be licensed by the CBK under Regulation 4.
  * Under Regulation 5(5), a DCP licence remains valid perpetually unless suspended or revoked by the CBK.
  * Under Regulation 5(6) and Second Schedule, an annual licence fee of KES 20,000 is payable on or before 31 December every year.
  * Under Regulation 5(7), a licensed DCP must submit to the CBK an annual return certifying compliance with the Act and Regulations on or before 31 December every year.
  * Consumer protection provisions prohibit unauthorized third-party contact, harassment, debt shaming, and abusive collection practices.
* **Prohibited / Unverified Claims:**
  * Do NOT refer to "annual licence renewal" or claim the licence expires on December 31.
  * Do NOT cite incorrect regulations (5(1), 5(2), 5(3), or 11 for annual returns).
  * Do NOT guarantee CBK application approval.
