## 2. Regulatory Evidence & Statutory Scope
* **Evidence Matrix References:** `ODPC Registration`, `ODPC Mandatory Threshold Exemptions`, `ODPC Certificate Renewal`, `Notifiable Data Breach`, `DPIA Obligation`.
* **Primary Statutory Instruments:**
  1. Data Protection Act, 2019 (Act No. 24 of 2019)
  2. Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021
  3. Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021
* **Approved Legal Claims:**
  * Mandatory registration applies to Data Controllers and Processors under Regulation 4 of the Registration Regulations 2021.
  * The small-entity exemption (turnover < KES 5M and employees < 10) does NOT apply where the processing falls within a Third Schedule mandatory-registration purpose, including the provision of financial services.
  * Registration certificate is valid for 24 months under Regulation 9, with renewal governed by Regulation 11.
  * Under Section 43(1) of the Data Protection Act 2019, a personal data breach is notifiable to the Data Commissioner without delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware only where personal data has been accessed/acquired without authorization AND there is a real risk of harm to the data subject.
  * Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA) are required under Section 31 DPA 2019 and Regulation 49 General Regs 2021 for high-risk processing operations.
* **Prohibited / Unverified Claims:**
  * Do NOT state "all data breaches must be reported within 72 hours" without the Section 43 harm standard.
  * Do NOT claim annual certificate expiry (validity is 24 months).
  * Do NOT cite obsolete Regulation 9(3) for renewal (use Regulation 11).
