## 1. 90-Day Phased Execution Plan

* **Operator Task 2:** Verify Google Search Console property (`sheriabot.com`) and submit `https://sheriabot.com/sitemap.xml`.

* **Query Performance Analysis:** Extract first 28-day GSC query report. Identify queries in positions 6–20 for title/meta CTR testing.

### Weeks 9–12: Authority Compounding, Conversion Optimization & Regional Planning
* **Backlink & Citation Audit:** Track earned referring domains and convert unlinked brand mentions.
* **CRO Assessment:** Analyze PostHog funnel metrics (`seo_authority_page_view` -> `seo_cta_click` -> `seo_register_complete`).
* **Regional Expansion Gate Review:** Evaluate Rwanda statutory corpus readiness against the Regional Readiness Gate (`SEO-S06-DOC-REGIONAL-006`).

## 2. Weekly & Monthly Operating Review Cadence

### Weekly Operating Review (30 Minutes)
1. **Search Console Health:** Review Page Indexing report for new unindexed URLs or 404/500 crawl errors.
2. **Freshness Invariant:** Review Kenya Gazette weekly notices for newly gazetted financial sector legal notices.
3. **Core Metric Check:** Monitor week-over-week impression and click trends for Kenya authority clusters.

### Monthly Executive Review (60 Minutes)
1. **Cluster Ranking & Impression Distribution:** Evaluate performance of core pillars (CBK, ODPC, AML/CFT, Sandbox).
2. **Link Profile Quality:** Review new referring domains against the Link Monitoring Policy (`SEO-S05-DOC-MONITORING-008`).
3. **CRO Conversion Attribution:** Review assisted signups and enterprise demo requests originating from organic landing pages.
4. **Content Lifecycle Decisions:** Approve content updates for spokes exceeding the 180-day review threshold.
