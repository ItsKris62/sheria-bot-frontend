## 1. User-Intent First Hierarchy

In accordance with SheriaBot's editorial principles, regulatory pages prioritize informational trust before commercial conversion:

```text
1. DIRECT STATUTORY ANSWER (Top QuickAnswer Box)
        ↓
2. STATUTORY EVIDENCE & PRIMARY CITATIONS
        ↓
3. ACTIONABLE OPERATIONAL GUIDANCE & REQUIREMENTS
        ↓
4. CONTEXTUAL SHERIABOT COMPLIANCE TOOL BRIDGE (Bottom CTA)
```

**Prohibited Patterns:**
* No popup interstitials blocking reading.
* No forced account registration before viewing statutory guides.
* No countdown timers or artificial scarcity banners.

## 3. SEO Analytics Funnel Event Taxonomy

* **`seo_authority_page_view`:** Fired when a user loads an authority pillar, spoke, or hero linkable asset.
* **`seo_kb_page_view`:** Fired when a user views a static operational knowledge base guide.
* **`seo_cta_click`:** Fired when a user clicks the bottom conversion bridge button on any SEO article.
* **`seo_register_start`:** Fired when an organic visitor navigates to `/register`.
* **`seo_register_complete`:** Fired when an organic visitor successfully creates a startup or enterprise account.
