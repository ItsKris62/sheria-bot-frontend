Track the following core brand identifiers across public search engines, news alerts, and social platforms:
1. `"SheriaBot"`
2. `"Sheria Bot"`
3. `"sheriabot.com"`
4. `"Sheria-Bot"`

---

## 2. Unlinked Brand Mention Recovery Protocol

When an editorial publication, tech directory, or community blog mentions SheriaBot without a hyperlink:
1. **Context Evaluation:** Verify that the mention is topically relevant and neutral/positive.
2. **Contact Identification:** Find the author or editorial contact via public publication mastheads.
3. **Value-Added Outreach:** Send a brief, polite note thanking them for the mention and offering a relevant deep link (e.g. to the Regulatory Change Tracker or Kenya FinTech Compliance Hub) to enrich their readers' experience.
4. **Tracking:** Log all outreach activities in the prospect register to prevent redundant contact.
