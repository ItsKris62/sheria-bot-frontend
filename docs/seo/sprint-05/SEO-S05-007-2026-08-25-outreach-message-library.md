## 1. Outreach Standards & Anti-Spam Rules

* **Mandatory Personalization:** Every outreach message must reference a specific recent article, academic curriculum, or member initiative.
* **Value-First Approach:** Always offer an original, useful resource (e.g. Regulatory Change Tracker, FinTech Compliance Calendar) before discussing citations.
* **Zero Aggressive Follow-up:** Maximum of ONE polite follow-up after 5–7 business days if no response is received.
