## 2. Regulatory Status Taxonomy Definitions

* **Statutory Legislation / Enacted Regulations:**
  - `IN_FORCE`: Enacted and currently in active operational effect.
  - `GAZETTED`: Officially published in the Kenya Gazette; awaiting commencement date.
  - `PROPOSED`: Draft bill or consultative draft not yet passed into law.
  - `AMENDED`: Modified by subsequent amendment regulations.
  - `REPEALED` / `SUPERSEDED`: Revoked or replaced by newer legislation.
* **Supervisory Policy & Guidance:**
  - `ACTIVE_GUIDANCE`: Official operational guidance or sandbox guidelines currently applied by the regulator.
  - `SUPERSEDED_GUIDANCE`: Replaced by updated supervisory notes.
  - `WITHDRAWN_GUIDANCE`: Formally discontinued by regulatory circular.
